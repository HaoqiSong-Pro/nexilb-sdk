# NexiLB research-use application privacy notice

Notice version: `NexiLB-Privacy-Notice-1.0`  
Effective date: 2026-09-01  
Contact: Haoqi Song, `haoqisong@126.com`

## Scope and purpose

This notice applies only to personal information sent for a NexiLB
research-use application. The information is used to identify and contact the
applicant, assess the requested non-commercial research scope, prevent scope or
access ambiguity, communicate a decision, prepare a separate written
authorization when approved, and retain the minimum decision record described
below. It is not used for advertising, applicant profiling or an automated
licensing decision.

The application channel is ordinary email, not an online form or automated
endpoint. Email is not represented as end-to-end encrypted. Applicants must
not send sensitive or unrelated material and should use the minimum-data form
in `OFFLINE-APPLICATION.md`.

## Information collected

The application requests the applicant's name, email, organization or
independent status, role, country or region, project title and research
summary, requested NexiLB version and artifacts, platform, requested term,
named users, installation count, intended outputs, external collaborators and
the required acknowledgements. No government identifier, postal address,
telephone number, payment information, credentials, health information,
research dataset or unrelated personal information is requested.

If clarification is necessary, only information material to the authorization
decision should be requested. Unsolicited excess information should not be
copied into the decision record.

## Access, service providers and transfers

Haoqi Song is the application contact and decision maker. Application data is
not sold. It may be processed by the email and storage providers needed to
receive, secure and retain the correspondence, or disclosed when required by
applicable law. Those providers may process or back up email in other
jurisdictions under their own service terms; this notice does not claim control
over a provider's independent backup schedule.

## Retention schedule

- A withdrawn application, or an incomplete application closed without a
  decision, is removed from the active decision record within 30 calendar days
  after written withdrawal or closure.
- A declined application and its decision correspondence are retained for no
  more than 12 months after the decision, then removed from the active record.
- An approved application, the resulting authorization and material decision
  correspondence are retained for the authorization term and three years
  after its expiration or termination, then removed from the active record.
- A shorter period will be used when the record is no longer necessary and no
  authorization, dispute, security incident or applicable legal obligation
  requires retention.

Deletion from the active record does not guarantee immediate deletion from an
email provider's disaster-recovery backup. No application data is intentionally
published as part of the SDK, documentation or release artifacts.

## Correction, withdrawal and questions

To request access to or correction of the application record, email
`haoqisong@126.com` from the applicant email with subject
`[NexiLB privacy request]` and identify the applicant and project. Verification
limited to what is reasonably necessary to protect the record may be required.

To withdraw a pending application, use subject
`[NexiLB application withdrawal]`. A correction or withdrawal request will be
acknowledged and acted on within 30 calendar days unless additional
verification or an applicable obligation prevents completion; any limitation
will be explained. Withdrawal of a pending request does not revoke or amend a
separate written authorization already issued.

## Notice changes

A revised notice receives a new version and effective date. The application
descriptor identifies the version supplied with each application. A material
change is not treated as retroactive consent; an applicant will be asked for
updated acknowledgement when the changed handling is needed for a pending
request.
