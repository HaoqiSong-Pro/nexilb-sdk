Privacy notice: `NexiLB-Privacy-Notice-1.0`  
Submission channel: ordinary email to `haoqisong@126.com`

This form is a request for consideration only. Submitting it, receiving an
acknowledgement, discussing a project, possessing an SDK or downloading a
runtime grants no right to use NexiLB. Permission exists only if Haoqi Song
later issues a separate written research-use authorization identifying the
approved applicant, project, version, artifacts and scope.

## Before submitting

`WRITTEN-AUTHORIZATION-TEMPLATE.md`. Send only the information requested
below. Do not send passwords, access tokens, private keys, government identity
documents, payment data, health data, unpublished datasets, third-party
confidential material or source code.

An incomplete form, a `NO` acknowledgement or a request outside the stated
research-use boundary may be declined without further processing. Additional
information may be requested, but the applicant should continue to apply the
same data-minimization rule.

## Decision and authorization boundary

The response may be a request for clarification, a decline, or a separate
written authorization. Only the last of these grants the rights expressly
listed in it. An authorization must have an authorization identifier, identify
the applicant and project, state the exact version and artifact set, define the
term and restrictions, and be issued by Haoqi Song. Silence, an automated
delivery receipt and an acknowledgement of receipt are not approvals.

To correct an application or withdraw it before a decision, email
`haoqisong@126.com` from the applicant email with subject
`[NexiLB application correction]` or `[NexiLB application withdrawal]` and
identify the applicant and project. Withdrawal stops the pending request; it
does not alter a separately issued authorization, whose own amendment and
termination terms control.
